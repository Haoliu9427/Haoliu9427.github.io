PROFILE / ABOUT

Personal profile

Research at the intersection of artificial intelligence, marine ecology, and global change.

My work asks how marine and aquatic ecosystems respond to interacting environmental pressures — and how AI can help detect, explain, and anticipate those changes.

With training across applied mathematics, computer science, and environmental science, I combine predictive modelling with interpretable analysis, causal reasoning, and graph-based representations. The goal is not only to improve model performance, but to produce evidence that can support ecological understanding and environmental decision-making.

研究思路

让计算方法回应真实的生态问题

01

从生态问题出发

研究从具体的环境或生态问题出发，而不是先选定一个模型，再寻找应用场景。

02

根据问题选择方法

结合研究问题和数据特点，选择预测模型，并在需要时引入可解释分析、时滞分析、因果推断以及空间或图结构方法。

03

区分预测与解释

模型预测表现、统计关联和机制解释属于不同层面的证据。
